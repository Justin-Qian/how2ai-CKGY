The paper introduces PEER (parameter-efficient expert retrieval), a new type of layer for transformer models that uses sparse mixture-of-experts (MoE) architectures to decouple model size from computational cost. PEER uses a lightweight design for experts, poorly understood in MoE models, and a product key retrieval technique to efficiently select experts from a large pool. The technique improves the performance-compute trade-off compared to dense feedforward layers and coarse-grained MoEs. The paper also investigates the influence of various design choices for PEER, such as the number of experts and query batch normalization. The experiments showed that PEER can unlock the potential for further scaling of transformer models while maintaining computational efficiency.
